About

Three decades of getting care to people, wherever they are.

I am Pascaline Wolfermann, an independent advisor in global healthcare intelligence and medical logistics, and the founder of Pelagos Global.

My career began far from any boardroom. In 1993 I joined Médecins du Monde in Iquitos, Peru, as administrator and logistician for emergency medical assistance during a cholera outbreak in the Amazon basin. Moving supplies, teams and patients against the clock and the geography of the river taught me what international health assistance really demands.

I went on to international services and product management at GlobalCare, then spent almost a decade at FrontierMEDEX as Resource Development Manager, building the provider relationships that travel-assistance and crisis-support programs rely on when something goes wrong abroad.

After FrontierMEDEX became part of UnitedHealth Group, I spent thirteen years at Director level leading health intelligence: first Health, Aero-Medical & Logistics Intelligence for Global Assistance, then Global Solutions Healthcare Intelligence at UnitedHealthcare Global. My team ran a worldwide quality assessment program for medical and transport providers, monitored country health conditions, and produced tailored intelligence such as medical emergency response plans for large events and remote locations.

In 2025 I went independent and founded Pelagos Global, named for the open sea, to bring that experience directly to the organisations responsible for people abroad. The same year I co-authored a peer-reviewed paper on international aeromedical transport in the Air Medical Journal. My grounding in international relations and languages at Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne still shapes how I work: across borders, cultures and systems.
