Peer-reviewed publication Air Medical Journal · 2025

Don’t Pay the Ferryman: International Aeromedical Transport of Patients to or From Treatments Prohibited in Their Place of Residence

An examination of the ethical, legal and operational questions raised when patients are flown across borders to reach, or return from, treatments that are not permitted where they live. Pascaline contributed as co-author, drawing on her ethics work in the aeromedical community.
